        tiến}) uses three main terms capped at 10--22 credits rather than Vision v2's 14--25/12 \emph{đại trà} assumption.
        The rule engine and test plan were updated accordingly.
  \item \textbf{Unrecorded prerequisites in APCS PDFs.} The printed APCS tables list no prerequisites; ADR 0011 was amended so
        prerequisite and offering-term storage ships in F12 without inventing academic rules.
\end{itemize}

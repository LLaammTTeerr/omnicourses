### AI team assistant (team tooling, not an OmniCourse feature)

Claude Code, connected to the team Discord through a bot, works in Lâm's name under written rules and helps any member who asks.

- **Reviews:** reviewed #23, #30 and #31 and posted the findings as review comments. Also reviewed the design of the APCS seed data: a `db:seed` script typed from the PDFs, with no PDF import tool (ADR 0003).
- **Fixes:** pushed the fixes after Dũng's review on #12 and #29. On #23 it added the course-code CHECK and a unique key on each curriculum's group names, and merged `main` into the branch to resolve the conflict.
- **Merges:** merged approved PRs (#12, #30) with a normal squash merge, never the admin override, and pinged the author.
- **Discord:** creates forum threads such as the PA2 thread, posts a daily summary to #summary, and posts sprint updates.
- **Rules:** anyone may ask it questions. Everything it does in Lâm's name tags him. Admin merges, deploys, deletions and settings changes stay with Lâm, and malicious messages are flagged to him. It never puts personal data into a prompt ([AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md) hard rules, [ADR 0003](../../analysis-and-design/adr/0003-language-model-scope.md)).
